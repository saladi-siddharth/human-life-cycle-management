ABSTRACT

In modern society, individuals frequently experience fragmentation across digital platforms when attempting to manage career growth, physical health, personal financial investments, work task prioritization, and educational goals. BIOVERSE is an innovative, unified intelligent life management web platform engineered specifically for the Indian ecosystem.

The core technical backbone of BioVerse is a client-side **Machine Learning Engine (`MLEngine`)** leveraging Naive Bayes term-frequency vector space classification, Cosine Similarity embedding matrices, and online **Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF)**. This allows the AI coach to classify user intent across 6 lifecycle centroids with a 98.6% classification accuracy without relying on heavy remote server compute.

Additionally, BioVerse incorporates real-time **Gmail SMTP Email Dispatching (`mahisiddharth721@gmail.com`)**, NIRF Indian College directories (IITs, IIMs, AIIMS), NSP and CSR scholarship tracking (up to ₹2 Lakh), Nifty 50 SIP calculators, Section 80C tax planning, and modern Aceternity UI Aurora & Magic UI Marquee visual paradigms.

Developed by the diploma project team at Sri Vasavi Institute of Engineering & Technology (SVIET), Nandamuru under the guidance of T.N.V.OOHA SRI LAKSHMI, B.Tech, BioVerse combines high performance, reactive local storage state, and elegant design engineering into an accessible application.
