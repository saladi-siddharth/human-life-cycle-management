Data Silos
Career goals, workouts, bank accounts, and daily tasks exist in completely disconnected databases.
📱
App Fatigue
Switching between 8 separate apps daily causes friction, duplicated manual data entry, and abandonment.

Bloated Runtimes

Ad Distractions
Conventional tools monetize attention with invasive commercial advertisements and aggressive paywalls.
